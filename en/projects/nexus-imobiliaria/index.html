Nexus Imobiliária

A real estate platform with a dashboard for agents and map-based search for the public. It is Rede Imobiliária, the white-label SaaS I'm developing at Hex Labs.

Challenge
Real estate agents of all ages need to list and update properties without relying on a tech person; home seekers want to see the location and surroundings without drowning in information.
What I did
Research and journeys, personas, the agent dashboard, team permission levels, map search and the build in Next.js and Supabase.
Outcome
A product built end to end, from research to code, as a showcase for Hex Labs.

01 / Context

The problem

Agents of all ages need to add and update listings without relying on someone from IT. On the other side, people looking for a property want to see the location and the surroundings without getting lost in too much information.

02 / Process

Research and journey

The work started with the journey of people who buy or rent and of people who list properties. Then came the prototype and the flowchart of how the interfaces connect.

03 / Process

Personas

Three personas guided the design: Márcia, a real estate agent; Karla, who is looking for an apartment; and Pablo, who wants to rent out his house. Since the other two depend on Márcia's work, the design started with her dashboard.

04 / Process

The agent's dashboard

Adding a listing is the agent's most frequent task, so the new listing button is the focal point of the dashboard. The listing form has an ad preview, edits go live on save, and the analytics page shows times, devices, and the most-viewed properties.

05 / Process

Team and permissions

Three access levels: staff (sees performance and suggests edits), agent (also manages listings), and manager (also adds team members). A history shows each person's latest actions.

06 / Process

Search for the public

Search shows up right at the entrance of the site. Results come with a map of where the properties are, and the property page leads straight to the agent's WhatsApp.

07 / Outcome

Where it landed

  • A product built end to end, from research to code, as a Hex Labs showcase.
