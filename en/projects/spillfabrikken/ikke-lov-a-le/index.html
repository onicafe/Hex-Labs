Ikke lov å le

“No laughing allowed”

“Ler du, taper du!”

You laugh, you lose!

A game based on Mads Hansen's VGTV series “Ikke lov å le”, in which comedians try to make the others laugh. Whoever laughs loses. The cards are split into four challenges, each with its own color: Stand-up, Imitasjon (impressions), Challenges, and Jodel (yodeling).

Client
SpillFabrikken, Trondheim, Norway
My role
Proposed box and category cards
My time at the publisher
Apr 2020 – Sep 2021
Tools
Photoshop and Illustrator

01 / The game

How to play

  1. 1

    On your turn, draw a card and do the challenge: stand-up, an impression, a challenge, or a yodel.

  2. 2

    The other players try not to laugh.

  3. 3

    Whoever laughs loses the round.
