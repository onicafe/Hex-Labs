Jentekveld

“Girls' night”

“Perfekte spillet for jentekvelder”

The perfect game for girls' nights

A game for girls' nights, with four card categories, a small ball, and a sand timer. The identity draws on the world of women's beauty: pink marble and a handwritten logo.

See it in the SpillFabrikken store

SpillFabrikken, Trondheim, Norway
My role

My time at the publisher
Apr 2020 – Sep 2021
Tools

01 / The game

How to play

  1. 1

    “Dropp gutta, det er tid for Jentekveld!”: ditch the guys, it's time for girls' night.

  2. 2

    The cards are split into Dilemma, Fem Fingre (five fingers), 100 Spørsmål (100 questions), and Hot Seat.

  3. 3

    No points and no winner: just do what the card says.

02 / Behind the scenes

Context and decisions

  • The person who brought the idea was inspired by Norwegian women's beauty products, especially packaging with a marble texture.

  • I recreated the marble texture in vector and built a sophisticated look on top of it, designed for a female audience.
