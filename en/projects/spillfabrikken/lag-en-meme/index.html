Lag en Meme

“Make a meme”

“Et spill for oss som elsker memes”

A game for those of us who love memes

Each player makes a meme by pairing one of their phrases with the round's image. The stand holds the image upright in the middle of the table, front and center.

01 / The game

How to play

  1. 1

    An image card goes on the stand, in the middle of the table.

  2. 2

    Each player picks the funniest caption for the image from their text cards.

  3. 3

    The round's judge picks the best meme and awards the point. The judge role passes clockwise. Whoever collects 10 cards wins.
