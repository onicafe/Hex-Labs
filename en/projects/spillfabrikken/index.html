SpillFabrikken

“The game factory”

A Norwegian publisher of card and party games. On a small team, I was the sole designer: I received each game's concept, designed the cards and boxes, helped define the rules, and delivered print-ready artwork to the factory. In a year and a half, eight game projects joined the line.

02 / Role

The publisher's sole designer

  • As the sole designer, I handled all visual production: identity, cards, boxes, rulebooks, mockups, ads, and website assets.

  • The rules were split with the publisher: some came ready-made, others I proposed, and some of my proposals made it into the games.

  • Card text came from the publisher, with suggestions from me.

  • I coordinated two outside collaborators: a 3D modeler, whom I recommended and who worked with me on a few projects, and an illustrator.

03 / Process

From brief to factory

  1. 01

    Briefing

    The publisher sent the game concept: the idea, the audience, and sometimes the rules.

  2. 02

    Photoshop prototype

    First versions of the box and card as mockups, to agree on the visual direction with the publisher.

  3. 03

    Cards and box

    Once the card count was set, I worked directly with the factory in China, which sent the box dimensions and dieline.

  4. 04

    Print-ready artwork in Illustrator

    The approved art was rebuilt in vector and fitted to the factory's dieline, including the inside of the box.

  5. 05

    Card by card

    For games with 300 to 400 cards, I built sheets with several cards each and laid out every text individually.

  6. 06

    Proof and production

    The factory made a test sample. Once it was approved, the game went into mass production.

The pace was fast: some boxes were finished over a single weekend.

04 / Brand

Visual identity

The symbol comes from the diamonds suit of a deck of cards: a red diamond that holds the initials SP·FK. Next to the name it works as the dot of an i, and on its own it becomes the seal on the boxes and cards. The presentation covers its construction, variations and a reduction test down to 10% of its size.
