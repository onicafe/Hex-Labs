“Paranoia”

“The game to put all friendships to test”

The game that puts every friendship to the test

A “who here is most likely to…” question game, with truth cards and penalty cards. The project was cancelled, and the idea became another game from the publisher. This page shows the visual direction I developed.

Client
SpillFabrikken, Trondheim, Norway
My role
Visual direction, logo, cards, and box
My time at the publisher
Apr 2020 – Sep 2021
Tools
Photoshop and Illustrator

01 / The game

How to play

  1. 1

    The cards ask questions like “Who's the most likely to commit a crime?”

  2. 2

    Sannhet (truth) cards have revealing questions and challenges.

  3. 3

    Taper (loser) cards set the penalty for whoever loses the round.
