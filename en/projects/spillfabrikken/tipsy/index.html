Tipsy

“Slightly drunk,” and also a tower that tips over

“Trekk, bygg, drikk, gjenta!”

Pull, build, drink, repeat!

A block tower for a drinking game: 51 wooden blocks, each with a printed challenge. It was the most unusual project in the line, because we moved from cards to a physical object.

See it in the SpillFabrikken store
Client
SpillFabrikken, Trondheim, Norway
My role
Design of every block, box, rulebook, and direction of the 3D renders
My time at the publisher
Apr 2020 – Sep 2021
Tools
Photoshop and Illustrator

01 / The game

How to play

  1. 1

    Build the tower and, on your turn, pull out a block.

  2. 2

    Do the challenge printed on the block.

  3. 3

    Place the block on top and pass the turn without knocking the tower over. Your hand gets less steady every round.

02 / Behind the scenes

Context and decisions

  • I drew each block by hand in Illustrator, with its own color and icon for each challenge.

  • We made two versions: a red, more adult one, which went to the Norwegian market, and a lighter one in pale tones, with a different slogan.

  • The name plays on the double meaning of “tipsy”: being slightly drunk, and a tower that wobbles.

Credits

  • 3D renders: a partner modeler, in Blender, working from my instructions. I recommended the modeler, wrote the brief, and coordinated the work.

03 / Exploration

Two versions

The red one, “Trekk, bygg, drikk, gjenta!”, made it to the store. The light one, “Bygge, mislykkes, drikk. Gjenta!” (build, fail, drink, repeat), has a softer tone.
