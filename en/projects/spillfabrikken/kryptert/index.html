Kryptert

“Encrypted”

“Ett spill som får deg til å snakke”

A game that gets you talking

The publisher's first game. Each card has a phrase that makes no sense on paper but, read aloud, turns into a familiar expression. According to SpillFabrikken, it has been Norway's best-selling card game for three years running. The same card and box system led to a family of editions: Christmas, 18+, and family.

01 / The game

How to play

  1. 1

    One player reads the card's coded phrase aloud, such as “tha koffe redd akk”.

  2. 2

    The others try to work out what the phrase means from its sound: in this case, tacofredag (taco Friday).

  3. 3

    In teams or individually, whoever decodes the most cards before the sand timer runs out wins.

02 / Behind the scenes

Context and decisions

  • The original idea for the game came from the publisher. The later editions came out of brainstorms between us.

  • Some came from the cards themselves: we had written 18+ phrases that didn't fit in the original edition and decided to turn them into an extra pack of their own.

  • I laid out the cards one by one from the phrase lists the publisher sent, and delivered them to the factory in templates with bleed and rounded corners.

04 / System

Anatomy of a card

12345
  1. 1

    Tab

    Game name in the category color, so the deck can be sorted at a glance.

  2. 2

    Answer

    The decoded expression, in heavy, centered type.

  3. 3

    Hint

    A “hint” band in the category color, to get a stuck round moving.

  4. 4

    Comment

    A line of humor that gives each card its own personality.

  5. 5

    Badge

    Category icon. The game has three: Grov, Fest, and Aktuelt.

06 / In use

Front and back, in hand

The front shows only the coded phrase, in a solid color and large type, so it can be read from across the table. The back has the answer. “Teki laff ula” becomes tequila-fylla (a tequila bender), and “Er mann full løs veg” becomes Herman Flesvig, a Norwegian comedian.

07 / Production

Files for the factory

Card templates for each category and the dieline for the two-piece box, with the artwork already fitted. The dieline went through five revisions with the factory before the final version.

08 / Packaging

Two-piece box

A red lid with a line pattern that suggests scrambled code. Inside, the box is black, with the phrase “BRA SPILL!” (good game!).

09 / Launch

Launch materials

A thank-you card that went inside the box, with a discount coupon for the next purchase. Also a Facebook cover and a social media filter with a coded phrase.

10 / Family

One card, four editions

The card and box structure stays the same. The color, pattern and tone of the phrases change, which makes it possible to launch new editions without redesigning the product from scratch.
